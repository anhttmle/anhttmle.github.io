### The Definition of a Business Model: 
It is how you create value, deliver it, make money, and sustain the business.

### The RSVP Framework: 
To build a successful model, think of it as an 'RSVP'—ensuring your model is Repeatable, Scalable, Valuable, and Defensible.
- Repeatable
- Scalable
- Valuable
- Defensible

### Finding the Core: 
Focus on a single, compelling 'Call'—the Capability of really Exceptional Value that defines your product.
